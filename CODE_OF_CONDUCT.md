# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the product and its operators

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the repository owner through a private GitHub security
advisory or a private message to a maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project owners.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
